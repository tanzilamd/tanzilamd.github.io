## A small tool from a familiar world
Debating gave me a practical place to build software for people whose workflow I understood. Tanzilbot was a Discord debate assistant bringing common tournament tasks into a familiar community space.

The project brought together speech timing, Bangla and English debate motions, and Google Sheets integration with Tabbycat tournament workflows.

## Where building and debating meet
This earlier work reflects a recurring interest: making a tool around a real activity rather than starting with a technology and looking for somewhere to use it.

The club announcement is linked as part of the project's history. This archive page does not imply that a hosted bot is currently available.
