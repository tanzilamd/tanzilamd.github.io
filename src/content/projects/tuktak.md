## A place for the small things
Tuktak is a Bangla-first, text-only social platform for Bangladeshi students and people connected to student life. The idea is simple: a thought does not need a large stage to start a worthwhile conversation.

The short-post format puts words first. Institution and education details are optional, so participation does not depend on turning a personal profile into an academic résumé.

## Building around the language
Bangla is part of the product's foundation, rather than a translation added at the end. The project uses self-hosted Hind Siliguri typography and a focused interface to support a comfortable reading and writing experience.

Underneath, Next.js, React and TypeScript structure the application. Supabase provides the authentication and data foundation, with database policies and validation part of the engineering approach.

## What matters in this work
- A clear, compact space for text-led conversations.
- A Bangla-first experience with readable typography.
- Optional identity details and deliberate privacy boundaries.
- A maintainable foundation for a product that can keep evolving.
