Through Bangladesh Scouts, I participated in social work and community activities, including blood donation campaigns. Scouting gave me opportunities to work with others, take responsibility, and help with practical needs in the community.

On Learn with Tanzil, I produced more than 25 Bangla videos about coding, HTML, and technology. I enjoy sharing what I learn in a way that helps someone else get started.

With Phulkuri Ashar, I took part in children’s welfare campaigns and fundraising, and taught basic math and English.
