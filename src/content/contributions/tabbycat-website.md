### What is Tabbycat?
Tabbycat helps organizers run debating tournaments: matching teams for rounds, assigning judges, collecting results, and publishing standings. It supports British Parliamentary and several two-team debate formats.

### My contribution
I designed and developed the Tabbycat software website in 2022. The Tabbycat Debate Association included my implementation in its official website, and the page credits “Designed by Tanzil Ahmed.”
